This is an E-Commerce shopping web application where users can register, log in, browse products with discounts, add/remove items to/from their cart, view account details, and log out.
The admin can manage products by adding new items to the store.

Features
User Registration: Users can create an account by providing necessary details like name, email, username, password and contact.
User Login: Registered users can log in to their accounts to access shopping features.
Product Catalog: Users can browse a list of available products, each with their respective price and discount.
Add to Cart: Users can add products to their shopping cart.
View Cart: Users can view the contents of their shopping cart at any time and the cart also display the total amount of the products in the cart.
Remove from Cart: Users can remove products from their cart before checkout.
User Account Info: Users can view and update their account information, such as name, email, and contact.
User Logout: Users can log out of their account for security and privacy.
